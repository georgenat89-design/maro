Free Cam > Camera > FOV controls the detached camera's field of view, from 10 to
155 degrees (default 90). It updates on the next render without restarting Free Cam.
The value is saved with the module and included in single-module public configs.

Free Cam's lens takes precedence over the normal game FOV and Custom FOV while it
is enabled. Key Zoom can still magnify that lens. Disabling Free Cam, leaving a world
or dying removes the override; the normal game FOV option is never changed.
The mouse wheel continues to control camera speed.
