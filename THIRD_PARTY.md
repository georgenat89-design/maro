# Bundled source and assets

- Trident Util, Boat Fly and Boat No Clip: adapted from 4ldenz's user-supplied Anubis 0.9.9+mc1.21.11 (`Anubis Beta 0.9.9.jar`, SHA256 `01e01b0935186bb4c7a67df97d540159ef1a62d4f1857019b757d0ad0327cadf`), GPL-3.0-only, with its license retained in `LICENSES/GPL-3.0-Anubis.txt`. Trident Util retains charge scaling and optional dry use; adds native-server timing, use preflight and bounded correction backoff. Boat movement, collision handling and down/restore anti-floating sequencing are adapted to native Maro settings and mixins, with exclusive modes, disable/dismount cleanup and session resets. No original addon binaries are bundled. Full corresponding source accompanies the jar.
- Speed Mine, Break Delay and Flight: adapted from Meteor Client 1.21.11 build 85, GPL-3.0, SHA256 `7f8a5082ce6480b01db414e10d54cd3dc41bb0a262ab55b76b58bc256f9bbaba`; original notice retained in `LICENSES/GPL-3.0-Meteor.txt`. Includes the three mining modes, block filters, break cooldown/instant-break option, Flight abilities/velocity and normal/packet anti-floating modes. Modified for native Maro controls, nonpersistent activation, ability/effect cleanup, builder isolation and connection argument rewriting. The user-requested Anti-Cheat Off menu section is original Maro UI. Meteor is used only as a separate test companion and is not bundled in the production jar.

- Air Stuck: adapted from `AirStuckModule` and `MixinAirStuckLocalPlayer` in 4ldenz's user-supplied Anubis 0.9.9+mc1.21.11 (`Anubis Beta 0.9.9.jar`, SHA256 `01e01b0935186bb4c7a67df97d540159ef1a62d4f1857019b757d0ad0327cadf`), GPL-3.0-only. Original license retained in `LICENSES/GPL-3.0-Anubis.txt`. Modified for native Maro toggles, nonpersistent activation, session/death resets, local movement freezing and movement/tick-end packet suppression. Server teleports and other packets remain native. Full corresponding source accompanies the built jar.
- No Fall: adapted from `com.example.addon.modules.nofall` in Big jeff's user-supplied addon-template 0.1.0 (`addon-template-0.1.0_4.jar`, SHA256 `308abafe8e011fdf1087c801b6081ddf659a9073e2cad394e940887ac2b8d500`), with its embedded CC0 1.0 Universal notice retained in `LICENSES/CC0-JeffAddon.txt`. Preserves the source's three-block trigger, full movement packets, tiny alternating vertical offsets and four grounded-report recovery; adds disable/session resets and native connection argument replacement instead of recursive silent sends. No other modules or addon binaries are bundled.

- Chest Stealer and Chest Dumper: transfer ordering, priorities, timing and cursor options adapted from the user-supplied Custom Addon 0.1.0 (`custom-addon-0.1.0.jar`, SHA256 `d9d9a9fd439ef5731792da920da8428b3d04acc211f96b6458b1fad30447061b`). The supplied archive includes CC0 1.0 Universal in `LICENSE_custom-addon`, retained as `LICENSES/CC0-CustomAddon.txt`. Reworked for native Maro settings and vanilla shift-clicks, per-tick limits, real-container recognition, component-aware capacity checks, bounded rejection retries and coordination with Auto Builder and Order Dropper. No other addon modules or binary dependencies are included.

- Anti Vanish: adapted from Anubis by 4ldenz, recovered from the user-provided `Anubis Client Beta 0.9.8.jar` (embedded metadata `0.8.12+mc1.21.11`). Its detection logic, text helpers and shard lookup are in `dev.maro.anubis` under GPL-3.0-only; original license in `LICENSES/GPL-3.0-Anubis.txt`. Modified for Maro's client-thread packet hooks, HUD, notifications and editable staff names on 2026-10-04. Anubis's Staff List module is not included. Distributions containing the adapted code are covered by GPL-3.0-only; original MIT notices for Maro/Nathan components remain intact. Full corresponding Maro source is supplied alongside the built jar.

- Player ESP silhouette rendering: the entity render-state link, silhouette command queue, silhouette vertex providers and outline-framebuffer swap (`dev.maro.render.esp.Silhouette*`, `EntityRenderStateAccess`, and the `EntityRenderStateMixin`, `EntityRenderManagerMixin` and `PlayerEspWorldRendererMixin` mixins) are adapted from Meteor Client's 1.21.11 entity shader code (https://github.com/MeteorDevelopment/meteor-client), GPL-3.0. Modified 2026-10-05 for Maro's Player ESP. The `player_esp` shaders and module are original to Maro.
- Nathan 1.21.11 module source: `dev.maro.nathan`, adapted from the user's current Nathan source. Its MIT notice is retained in `LICENSE-Nathan`. Per-file notices remain attached to their respective source files; SpawnerProtect carries its original All-Rights-Reserved attribution.
- Logical Zoom code: retained MIT notice in `LICENSES/MIT-LogicalZoom.txt`.
- Poppins font: SIL Open Font License in `LICENSES/OFL-1.1-Poppins.txt`.
- Barlow Condensed font: SIL Open Font License in `LICENSES/BarlowCondensed-OFL.txt`.
- Inter font: original Maro notice in `src/main/resources/INTER_LICENSE.txt`.
- SnakeYAML 2.7: Apache License 2.0, bundled as a nested dependency with its original license notices. Used with safe loading to read LRCLIB word timestamps.

The local `dev.maro.runtime` package implements the module APIs on Fabric and Maro. It does not bundle Meteor Client or Orbit binaries. The `nameeprotect` resource namespace is retained so existing Nathan shaders, textures, and fonts resolve correctly.
